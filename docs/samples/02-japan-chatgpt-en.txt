# 🇯🇵 7 Days in Japan: Tokyo, Hakone & Kyoto

## Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo
- Land at Haneda Airport (afternoon) and take the train to Shinjuku
- Check in at your hotel in **Shinjuku**
- Evening stroll through Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) for yakitori
- 💡 Tip: Buy a Suica card at the airport

## Day 2: Tokyo – Old & New
- **Morning:** Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa (arrive before 9:00 to beat the crowds)
- Lunch at Nakamise Street snack stalls
- **Afternoon:** teamLab Planets, Toyosu (book tickets in advance)
- **Evening:** Shibuya Crossing and Shibuya Sky at sunset

## Day 3: Tokyo – Culture & Pop Culture
- Meiji Shrine and Yoyogi Park
- Takeshita Street in Harajuku
- Akihabara Electric Town
- Dinner: ramen in Ikebukuro

## Day 4: Hakone
- Take the Romancecar from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto (about 85 minutes)
- Hakone Open-Air Museum
- Ride the Hakone Ropeway over Owakudani
- Overnight at a ryokan with an onsen

## Day 5: Hakone → Kyoto
- Morning bath at the ryokan
- Shinkansen from Odawara to Kyoto (about 2 hours)
- Fushimi Inari Shrine – walk the torii gates
- Dinner in Gion

## Day 6: Kyoto
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (go early!)
- Tenryu-ji Temple
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
- Nishiki Market for lunch snacks
- Evening: Pontocho Alley

## Day 7: Departure
- Breakfast near Kyoto Station
- Train to Kansai Airport (Haruka Express, 75 min)
- Fly home
